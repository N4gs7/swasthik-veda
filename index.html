Returns and exchanges

7-day returns

You can return or exchange an eligible saree within 7 days of delivery. Silk sarees are delicate, so they must come back unused, with the original tags and packaging.

Damaged or wrong item

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with photos or the unboxing video. You get a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping.

Small colour differences between your screen and the fabric, and small irregularities in weave or zari, are natural in handloom silk and are not defects.

How to start a return

  1. Use Contact us below, email swasthikvedasilks@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 85550 63040 with your order number.
  2. Send photos of the saree, the tags and the packaging. We reply within 48 hours.
  3. If approved, we arrange a free pickup. We check the saree within 2 business days of it reaching us.

Refunds

Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to your bank account or UPI, or as store credit if you prefer. We start the refund within 2 business days of approving the return, and banks usually take 5 to 7 business days to credit it.

Exchanges

Exchange for another saree of equal value, or pay the difference for a higher-priced one.

Not returnable

  • Sarees that have been worn, washed, dry-cleaned or altered
  • Sarees with fall, pico or blouse stitching done at your request
  • Sarees without the original tags and packaging
  • Items marked final sale, unless they arrive damaged or wrong
